## Electricity: uploading your own CSV

Accepted by `POST /api/datasets/upload` (UI: *Manage electricity data*), up to 20 MB.

| Column | Accepted names / formats |
|---|---|
| Timestamp | one column named `timestamp`, `datetime`, `date_time`, `time_stamp`, `time`, `date` or `ts` - **or** separate `Date` + `Time` columns (UCI style). Example: `2024-01-31 14:00` |
| Value | energy per row: `kwh`, `wh`, `energy`, `consumption`, `usage`, `units`, ... - **or** power: `kw`, `w`, `power`, `Global_active_power` (converted to energy using the reading interval) |
| Circuits (optional) | `circuit_<name>_kwh` columns, used to say which circuit caused an anomaly |

Separator (`,` `;` tab) is detected automatically. Readings must be **hourly or finer** and cover **at least 14 days**.

### Preprocessing (`backend/app/engine/loader.py`)

1. Parse timestamps; unparseable rows are excluded and counted.
2. Remove negative values and duplicate timestamps (duplicates merge with the earlier row).
3. Convert units (Wh → kWh, kW/W → kWh per interval).
4. Resample to hourly totals; partially covered hours are scaled by coverage.
5. Values above 60 kWh in one hour are treated as impossible and removed.
6. **Missing values:** gaps of 3 hours or less are interpolated; longer gaps are left empty (dropped from analysis, not invented).
7. A data-quality report lists every fix (duplicates, negatives, impossible values, gaps) so nothing is changed silently.

Files are rejected with a clear message when there is no timestamp, the value column can't be identified, readings are coarser than hourly, or there are fewer than 14 days.

## Electricity: manual readings

Entered in the app (date, optional hour `HH:00`, kWh, source, notes). Limits: not in the future, not negative, ≤ 60 kWh per hour or ≤ 500 kWh per day. One figure per day or hour. Imported data wins for days it covers; manual readings fill every other day.

## Water: CSV format

Accepted by `POST /api/water/upload/preview` then `POST /api/water/upload` (UI: *Water > Water Usage Analytics > Manage water data > Add Water CSV*), up to 20 MB.

| Column | Accepted names / formats |
|---|---|
| Date / time | `date`, `day`, `reading_date`, `usage_date`, `timestamp`, `datetime`, ... Daily (`2026-08-01`) or hourly (`2026-08-01 07:00`) |
| Volume | a litres or kL column, e.g. `litres`, `water_usage_litres`, `water_usage_kl`; or a cumulative meter column (`reading`, `meter_index`, `cumulative`, `totaliser`) which is converted to usage by differencing |
| Unit | taken from the header when it says (`l`, `ltr`, `litres`, `liters` → L; `kl`, `kilolitres`, `m3`, `cubic` → kL); otherwise chosen in the preview |

Columns can be mapped by hand in the preview. Files whose columns look like electricity (`kwh`, `power`, `voltage`, ...) are refused as water.

Example (`data/samples/water_daily_sample.csv`):

```csv
date,litres
2026-08-01,588
2026-08-02,640
```

### Validation and missing values

The preview saves nothing and lists every row that won't be imported, by line number: unrecognised or future dates, missing or non-numeric amounts, negative or implausible values (> 3,000 L in an hour or > 30,000 L in a day), duplicate dates in the file. Dates already stored can be kept (`skip`) or replaced. **Missing days are not filled in** - totals and charts show only recorded days. All volumes are stored in litres; a day's total is the sum of its hourly readings when it has any, otherwise its daily reading.

## Synthetic test data and its limitations

`backend/app/engine/synthetic.py` builds a 75-day Indian household from circuit profiles (AC, geyser, kitchen, lighting, always-on, water pump) with daily and weekly patterns and noise, then injects labelled spikes, sustained increases and repeated loads. It is used by the validation lab and the tests.

Limitations: it is **idealised** - circuit behaviour is regular, there are no holidays, weather or occupancy changes, and the injected anomalies are clean step changes. Detection scores on it (100% recall and precision) are an upper bound; on the real UCI home recall is 67-89% for the same injected anomalies. See [ML_ANOMALY_DETECTION.md](ML_ANOMALY_DETECTION.md#validation).

## Privacy

Uploaded data is stored only in the server's SQLite database, scoped to the uploading account, and deleted with the account. No dataset, database or user data is committed to this repository.
