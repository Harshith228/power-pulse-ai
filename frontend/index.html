Features

Everything you need to understand a home's energy

From raw meter exports to explained alerts and a costed action plan, in one place.

Intelligent Anomaly Detection

A robust hour-by-hour baseline plus an Isolation Forest flags sudden spikes, sustained increases and repeated unusual loads.

Energy Consumption Analytics

Daily profiles, weekday vs weekend patterns, a weekly heatmap and always-on base load, all from your own readings.

Smart Usage Monitoring

An alert feed, severity trends and the hours when anomalies strike, with notifications for what matters most.

Explainable Alerts

Every alert says why: actual vs expected, z-score, model rarity, the hourly jump and which circuit caused it.

Energy Saving Recommendations

Actions matched to the appliance and time of day: geyser timing, AC set-points, pumps and standby loads.

Cost & Savings Analysis

An editable INR slab tariff prices every anomaly, estimates your typical bill and what you could save each month.

How It Works

From a CSV to clear answers in five steps

The whole pipeline runs in seconds on years of hourly data.

  1. 01

    Upload Dataset

    Drop a smart-meter CSV or load the public UCI dataset. Separators, units and timestamps are detected automatically.

  2. 02

    Analyse Consumption

    Readings are cleaned, resampled to hourly and profiled by hour, weekday and weekend.

  3. 03

    Detect Anomalies

    A 28-day robust baseline and an Isolation Forest score every hour; event rules group them into alerts.

  4. 04

    Generate Recommendations

    Each alert is explained and linked to a practical fix for the appliance involved.

  5. 05

    Understand Potential Savings

    Your tariff turns wasted kWh into rupees per month, so you know which fix pays off first.

About

Built to make household energy legible

Most homes only see one number: the monthly bill. PowerPulse looks at every hour, learns what normal means for that specific home, and points out the moments that cost money, with an explanation you can check.

It was built as a Tech Fest project for household energy anomaly detection. It runs entirely on your machine, keeps analyses in memory only, and is validated against labelled synthetic anomalies so its accuracy is measured, not claimed.

Open the Validation Lab
  • Measured accuracy

  • Private by default

    No accounts, no database. Results disappear when the server stops.

  • Made for Indian tariffs

    Slab-based pricing in INR, editable to match your DISCOM.

  • Honest about limits

    Detects increases only; needs 14+ days of hourly data. Estimates are labelled as estimates.
